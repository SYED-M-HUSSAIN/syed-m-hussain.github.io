## 🚀 Introducing OpenRobotics Lab – A Community Driven Learning Platform for Robotics 🤖

During the early stages of my robotics journey, I encountered a major hurdle: the lack of a unified, reliable platform for learning. Whether it was selecting the right programming language, identifying essential books, exploring simulation tools, or navigating the complexity of Robot Operating System (ROS), finding quality, structured resources was a challenge.

Over the years, through hands-on experience and countless hours of self-learning, I've gained a clearer understanding of what truly helps when learning robotics.

To give back to the community and support those on a similar path, I've built **OpenRobotics Lab**, an open-source platform that consolidates high-quality robotics resources into one accessible space.

## 🔍 Platform Highlights

- **Step-by-step tutorials** (beginner to advanced)
- **Curated robotics books, tools, and simulation resources**
- **Online course recommendations** from top institutions
- **Real-world project repositories** with code and documentation
